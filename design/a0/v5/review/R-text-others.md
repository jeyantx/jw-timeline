# R-text-others: text defects in files FIX-TEXT does not own (by FIX-TEXT, 22:03)

Most visible first. All year-tag and meta items come from `scripts/build_a0_sheet.py` → `year_tag()` unless noted.

1. **(303–303) Diocletian orders Scriptures burned** (row 3 turn, ≈ x 900, y 485), plus **(1–1)** Astrologers visit / Family flees to Egypt / Herod kills young boys (row 3, Jesus' birth area), plus **(1938–1938) 2,300 evenings and mornings** (row 4, ≈ x 600, y 625). A `between` record whose start and latest years are equal prints a range like "(303–303)". For "1 B.C.E. or 1 C.E." both years are 1 but the eras differ. The 2,300-days label should show 1938–1944 (the source date is "June 1 or 15, 1938, to October 8 or 22, 1944"), so the window end is being read as the start's latest. Fix in `year_tag()`: when the years are equal, print a single year; for 1 B.C.E./1 C.E., print "1 B.C.E./1 C.E." or similar; for spans, use `end`. **FIX-FILL**
2. **Meta says "year not stated" next to records that do state a year.** Examples: Stephen martyred (c. 33–34), Peter heals a lame beggar (33 or 34), Diocletian (303, Feb. 23–24), Hezekiah's Passover (746/745), Cyrus' decree (late 538 or early 537), and the 31-or-32 Galilee records. The meta `'year not stated · within'` is used for every `between` record and is shown as "year not stated" by `labW` in draw2.js. Suggest an empty meta, or "within" only. **FIX-FILL** (builder) or **FIX-LABELS** (`labW` mapping)

Only replace " to " between two numbers: `re.sub(r'(\d) to (\d)', r'\1–\2', …)`. **FIX-FILL**
