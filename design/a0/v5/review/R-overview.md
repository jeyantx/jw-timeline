# R-overview: the whole sheet at 1–2 m and up close

Shots: `/tmp/sw-overview-full.png` (Z 1.5), quadrants `/tmp/sw-overview-q1..q4.png` (Z 2.5), turns `/tmp/sw-overview-t2.png`, `/tmp/sw-overview-t3.png`, zoom `/tmp/sw-overview-z1.png`.

## Verdict in brief
- **From 1–2 m** you can read only the title, the photos, the gold line, the stats panel and the Paradise painting. Section headings (about 3 mm grey caps) and tier-1 labels (about 3–3.4 mm) cannot be told apart from tier-2 at that distance, so the "landmark" layer does not work.
- **Up close** it is rich and mostly clean. The smallest text is 1.9 mm em (about 5.4 pt, in `draw2.js`, `map.js`, `maps.js`). That is legible in print but at the limit, with the grey `C.g2` sub-lines the weakest. Strokes are 0.2 mm or more, which is fine.
- **Balance:** the top-right corner, the bottom strip under the maps and legend, and the world map are under-used. The left column and rows 1–3 are dense.

## Prioritised improvements (most visible first)

1. **No B.C.E./C.E. or row identity on the timeline itself.** `core.js` defines the row names ("ROW 1 · 4026–1200 B.C.E.", etc. plus a scale), but nothing draws them. A reader crossing turn 2 (x 0–190, y 305–430) sees "c. 18 Herod" and then "29 Jesus baptized" with no era switch shown. Draw a small row tag at each row start: row 1 at x≈192, y≈168; row 2 at x≈1030, y≈293; row 3 at x≈192, y≈418; row 4 at x≈1030, y≈543. Use the form "ROW 2 · 997–332 B.C.E. ←", and mark the B.C.E./C.E. switch on the turn-2 arc. *FIX-STRUCT* (`draw.js`, using `A0.ROWS[i].name`).
2. **Tier-1 landmarks are not clearly biggest.** Two examples: "1513 Red Sea deliverance" (x≈620, y≈134) and "1914– The last days" (x≈955, y≈497) are only slightly larger or bolder than tier 2, and "1804 British and Foreign Bible Society" (x≈1110, y≈485) reads as large as 1914. Make tier 1 ≥ 4.2 mm (about 12 pt) bold with the year in gold, keep tier 2 at about 2.9 mm, and audit which events carry tier 1. *FIX-LABELS* (`drawLabel`); tier list: *OWNER*.
3. **Section headings are too weak for wayfinding.** Examples: "THE JUDGES" (x≈795, y≈120), "THE LAST DAYS · …" (x≈750–893, y≈492), "THE HARVEST IS COMING" (x≈968, y≈492). At about 3 mm grey caps they vanish beyond 1 m. Raise them to 5–6 mm in ink, letter-spaced, as the second reading level after the title. *FIX-LABELS*, or *FIX-STRUCT* if they are drawn in `draw.js`.
4. **The row-4 era heading sits in row 3's label field.** "THE LAST DAYS · THE GOOD NEWS PREACHED IN ALL THE EARTH" (x 750–893, y≈492) and "THE FUTURE" (x≈266–295, y≈491) are placed among row-3 labels (Paul's journeys, Jesus' ministry). The dashed gold future-path line also runs through the heading at x≈700–760. Move both headings below the row-4 line, or give them a rule that ties them to row 4. *FIX-LABELS* / *FIX-PANELS* (future path).
5. **Row-4 stats lines reuse the series colours.** Inside the row-4 band (x 300–1100, y 535–575), "congregations" is blue (= kings of north/south), "pioneers" is orange (= world powers / copper), "Bible studies" is pink (= prophecy about a nation) and "peak publishers" is green (= messianic prophecy). In the overview these read as continuations of the prophecy lines. Use one neutral family for all stats lines (greys or one hue in tints) and keep the series hues for the series. *OWNER* (colour decision) → *FIX-STRUCT*.
6. **Stats value labels collide with the lines at the row-4 left edge.** "1,19,652", "17,43,567" and "76,03,182" (x 290–305, y 553–573) sit on top of the black, red and blue lines (see `z1`). Move them outside the band (x < 297) with a white halo. *FIX-STRUCT*.
7. **The direction of travel is unreadable from 1–2 m.** The direction chevrons on the history line are about 2 mm and sparse; row 2 and row 4 run right to left with nothing to show it at a distance. Add 5–6 mm gold chevrons on the gold line every ~120 mm, and one large arrow at each turn apex: x≈1150, y 243 (turn 1); x≈30, y 368 (turn 2); x≈1150, y 493 (turn 3). *FIX-STRUCT*.
8. **Turns do not clearly read as continuous time.** The turns change scale (judges "in order only", 2.4 yrs/mm, 1.2 yrs/mm), but nothing on the arcs says so. Turn 3 (x 1035–1189, y 430–555) also switches several series to dotted lines that the legend does not explain. Put the scale note from `core.js` along each arc in 2.2 mm italic, add 3–4 year ticks per arc, and add "dotted = …" to the legend. *FIX-STRUCT* (arcs), *FIX-MAP* (legend).
