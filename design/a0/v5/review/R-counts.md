## Defects, most visible first

1. **jw.org languages chart mixes three different measures (data error).** At x ≈ 817–977, y 68–85, the line runs 139 (2012, *navigable home-page* languages) → 508 (2013, *translation-production* languages; the CSV note says "not home-page languages") → 1,000 (2019, *content* languages). The climb it shows is not real. Cause: `scripts/build_a0_sheet.py` `growth()` keeps every confirmed row per year with no regard to the note/measure. Fix: drop the 2013 = 508 row from `web`. Ideally plot only the content-language points: 2019 = 1,000 (confirmed), 2022 = 1,000+ and 2023 = 1,070+ (approx, hollow). **FIX-FILL** (builder); measure choice is **OWNER**.
2. **jw.org chart stops at 2019 although the axis runs to 2026.** The right ~40% (x ≈ 920–977) is empty, and the headline reads "1,000 · 2019" even though the CSV has 2023 = 1,070 (approx). Cause: `growth()` line 148 `web = [... if ok]` drops all approx rows for this series, unlike NWT, which keeps them as hollow points. Keep approx rows here as hollow too, and show the value as "1,070+ · 2023". **FIX-FILL**
3. **"awaiting research" placeholder will print.** The Baptized chart at x ≈ 988–1035, y 44–61 has a hatched box with that text: data starts 1949 but the axis is forced to 1919. Start the axis at the first data year (1949), or drop the hatch and label it. Placeholder wording must not reach the A0 print. **FIX-PANELS** (`mini`, the `pend` branch; the call passes `pend=2024` but `mini` overrides it anyway).
4. **No y-scale anywhere.** Six charts have no gridline, no start value and no peak value. Readers can't tell 1 from 100 lakh, and the Memorial curve looks like "zero until 1950". Add the first value at the left end (e.g. "2,501 · 1899") and one light gridline with its value. **FIX-PANELS**
5. **Six different x-ranges in a small-multiples grid:** 1899, 1900, 1919, 1950, 2012 start years; 2026 and 2027 end years. Lining up in time across charts is impossible. Suggest one shared range (1914–2026) for Memorial, Lands, Baptized and Branches, and NWT ending at 2026, not 2027 (`mini(gx[0],gy[1],cw,chh,1950,2027,...)`). Also `mini` silently overrides `t0` with the first data year (`t0=Math.min(t0,data[0][0])`). **FIX-PANELS**; shared axis is **OWNER**.
6. **Branch offices: a straight line from 1900 (1) to 1942 (25)** at x ≈ 988→1040, y 88→85 suggests steady growth over 42 unresearched years. Break the line, or dash it, wherever the gap is more than 5 years. The same applies to Memorial 1899→1914→1919 and jw.org 2013→2019. **FIX-PANELS** (`mini` path: split `data` at gaps)
7. **Peak publishers 2025 (9,205,326 = 92 lakh), congregations (1,19,652), pioneers (17.4 lakh) and Bible studies (76 lakh) are not in this panel.** These are the headline 2025 numbers. They live only in the growth bands (draw2.js line ~170). Consider adding a 2025 total strip under the panel title: "92 lakh publishers · 1.20 lakh congregations · 241 lands · 2.06 crore at Memorial". **OWNER**
8. **Wasted space between the title and the panel.** x ≈ 420–640, y 20–96 (≈220 × 76 mm) is empty paper. Either widen the panel leftward (px 640 → ~440, charts ~230 mm wide each, or a 4×2 grid adding the 2025-total charts from item 7), or enlarge the title. **FIX-PANELS / OWNER**
9. **Too small for A0 viewing distance.** Axis years are 2.1 mm (~6 pt), headline values 2.5 mm, chart titles 2.65 mm, and the subtitle 2.65 mm. At 1–2 m these are hard to read. Suggest values at ≥4 mm (they are the point of the panel), titles at 3.2 mm and axis years at 2.6 mm. Charts are only 17 mm tall (`chh=17`) inside a 72 mm card. There is room for ~22 mm. **FIX-PANELS**
10. **Subtitle exposes process wording.** At x 645, y 35.5: "source: research return 14 Oct 2026" means nothing to a reader. Use "source: jw.org Service Year Reports and Yearbooks". **FIX-PANELS**
11. **Approx values in headlines are not marked.** "1,000 · 2019" comes from "1,000 languages" (ok), but if items 1–2 are fixed, the 2022/2023 values are "over 1,000" / "over 1,070" and need "+" or "≈" in the headline. 12. **Memorial values touch the top of the chart.** The 2021 point is 2.14 crore with `vmax=2.2e7`, so the line ends ~0.5 mm below the "2.06 crore · 2025" label baseline (x ≈ 795, y 44). Use `vmax 2.4e7`, or move the value label right/up. **FIX-PANELS**
