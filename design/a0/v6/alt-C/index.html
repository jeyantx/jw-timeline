What changed in this round
  1. Daniel 2: the large image is gone. A small panel in the left gutter shows each part, its kingdom, dates and scripture, and the metals also show as colour under the red world-power line.
  2. The stone: a dotted arc leaves the 1914 Kingdom station, crosses over Row 4 and strikes the line at Armageddon with a burst. The mountain panel is removed.
  3. The walking path: past Armageddon the red, blue and black rails fade one after another. The gold line carries on as the edge of a widening sandy path, with the 11 future milestones on either side and the Thousand Year Reign written inside it. The path runs into the paradise image slot, which fades in from the top.
  4. Images: 31 major events are marked for a round image slot (the fit report counts how many found room), and the throne and six creation days have slots too. Drag the jw.org artwork onto them on the full sheet; it is saved with the file.
  5. The opening follows Insight “Creation” and the NWT study notes on Col 1:15 and Re 3:14: Jehovah → the Word → the angels (Job 38:4-7) → the heavens, with the sun, moon and stars (Ge 1:1) → the formless earth (Ge 1:2) → Days 1–6 → Day 7. Each one is a station with an image slot.
  6. The turns are used like the rows. Each turn is a stretch of time: 1200–997 B.C.E. (the last judges, then Saul, David and Solomon as bars round the outside, with labels both inside and outside the curve), 332–2 B.C.E., and 1700–1870. Row labels can now run into the inside of a turn wherever the curve leaves room.
  7. Growth: Row 4’s track bed holds a log-scale curve of peak publishers, tagged at 1 lakh, 10 lakh, 50 lakh and 92 lakh (2025). Gridlines show 10, 100, 1,000 and 10,000, because early counts were not reported.
  8. Counts panel (top right): the 2025 totals in Indian numbering, and two language charts drawn on one shared time scale so that jw.org’s 13 years take a narrow strip.
  9. Modern milestones added: WBBR radio, phonograph talks, the Theocratic Ministry School, MEPS, the NWT Reference Bible, the Listen to God brochures, JW Library, JW Broadcasting, the study edition, jw.org in 1,000 languages, the pandemic ministry, the return to the public ministry, and the NWT in 350 languages. The main study books (Divine Plan → Enjoy Life Forever!) run as a relay of bars.
