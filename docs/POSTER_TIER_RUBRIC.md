# Priority tiers — rubric (poster and app)

Every record gets **one tier**. The A0 poster shows **all tier 1 and tier 2 records, and nothing below**. The rule is the same in every era. The app shows everything and can filter by tier.

| tier | name | meaning | poster |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Landmark | Defines its period. It would appear on any one-page summary of Bible or Kingdom history. | yes, large label |
| **2** | Major | Clearly important within its period. A regular Bible reader or meeting attender would recognise it. | yes, normal label |
| **3** | Notable detail | Real and sourced, but context. Researchers want it; a visitor does not need it. | no (app) |
| **4** | Fine detail / redundant | Already shown by a span bar (birth, death, accession), duplicates another record, or is a statistic, a one-line index entry or an administrative detail. | no (app) |

## Consistency rules (most important)

- **C1 · Like items get like tiers.** All ten plagues are one record and one tier. All kings' reigns share one tier, and so do all prophets' activity bars and all Paul's journeys. Miracles of the same fame get the same tier. If you give two similar records different tiers, the `why` must say what distinguishes them.
- **C2 · Judge the event, not the record's wording or source count.** A one-source record of a famous event outranks a five-source record of a minor one.
- **C3 · Use the era targets below to calibrate, not to force.** If your era clearly has more or fewer landmarks, say so in the report rather than bending the rubric.
- **C4 · People vs Kingdom (modern era).** From 1870 on, rank **Kingdom and organization activity**, as *God's Kingdom Rules!* presents it: preaching, publications, Bible translation, conventions, theocratic schools, legal victories, construction, refinements in understanding, and persecution and bans. **Individual persons' births, deaths, appointments and tenures go to tier 3–4.** Leadership changes go to tier 2 only when the organization itself changes, as in 1971–1976 when the Governing Body was enlarged and set up in committees.
- **C5 · Series steps.** The *core steps* of the highlight series are tier 1 or 2:
  - the 7 steps of NWT App. B1 (promised offspring);
  - the B9 rise and fall dates of the world powers;
  - the start and end of the 70 weeks and of the seven times;
  - when each covenant was made;
  - the wheat-and-weeds stages.

  Other series members are graded on their own merit.
- **C6 · Already decided by rule (do not re-grade):**
  - Points flagged `covered_by_span` → **4**, because their bar shows them.
  - Future records → **1**.
  - Reigns of the kings of Judah and Israel, Saul, David and Solomon → **2**.
  - Prophets' activity bars → **2**.
  - Judgeships → **2**.
  - World-power spans → **1**.
  - Prophetic-period spans → **1**.
  - Patriarch lifespans Adam–Joseph → **1**; other Bible-era lifespans → **2**; lifespans after 100 C.E. (reformers, Watch Tower presidents) → **3** (C4).
  - Watch Tower presidents' and Governing Body members' tenures → **3** (C4).
  - The 66 "writing completed" records from batch b11 → **2** (they form the Bible-books strip).
  - Publications Index one-line entries (`chart_hint = index_entry`) → **3** by default. Grade **2** only for an outstanding clarification, such as the 2013 understanding of the faithful slave.

