# A0 Wall Chart — Design Brief (Phase 1: concept wireframes)

**For:** Claude Design
**Deliverable now:** concept **wireframes** of a printed A0 timeline poster. Use no real data, only placeholder blocks, so the layout idea can be validated before anything is designed in detail.
**Not a web page.** This is a large printed chart for a wall, studied standing up, in the spirit of usefulcharts.com timelines but richer, more colourful, multi-series, and with images.

---

## 1. What the poster is

One sheet covering **all of Bible history and the modern history of Jehovah's Witnesses**: from the creation of Adam (4026 B.C.E.) through 2026, and on to the prophesied future (undated, in sequence).

It must show **comparisons and connections**, not just a list of dates:
- Lives overlapping. Methuselah's life overlaps Adam's and Noah's.
- Kings of Judah and Israel side by side, with the prophets who served under them.
- World powers rising and falling as Daniel foretold.
- Prophecies joined to their fulfillments across centuries. Genesis 3:15 points to 33 C.E. and to the future.
- The modern organisation growing year by year.

Audience: Jehovah's Witness families, students and speakers, both Tamil and English. Tone: beautiful, calm, trustworthy, a reference you are proud to hang. Not a dashboard and not a cartoon.

All facts come from jw.org publications. The poster is an **unofficial personal study chart**: no jw.org logo, branding or official look.

## 2. Physical format

| item | spec |
|---|---|
| Sheet | **A0 = 841 × 1189 mm.** Propose landscape or portrait; landscape is expected but not required. |
| Bleed / safe area | 5 mm bleed, 20 mm safe margin |
| Output (later phase) | Vector PDF (PDF/X-4), images at ≥ 300 dpi at placed size, CMYK-safe palette, matte paper |
| Viewing distances | **3 m:** title, eras and the shape of history are readable. **1 m:** period names, series names and major events are readable. **30 cm:** every label, date and scripture is readable. |
| Minimum type at 1:1 | Event label ≥ 9 pt, date/scripture ≥ 7.5 pt, era headings 60–90 pt, title 150–220 pt |
| Languages | English and Tamil (தமிழ்). Tamil runs about 20–30 % longer and needs more line height. See §8. |

## 3. Three levels of reading (the core design goal)

1. **3-second read, from across the room:** 6,000 years of history, four great eras, "you are here" in 2026, and the future still ahead.
2. **30-second read:** the major periods (patriarchs, Israel in Egypt, the judges, the kings, exile and return, Jesus, the apostles, apostasy and the Bible's survival, modern history, the future) and the seven world powers.
3. **5-minute read:** the coloured **series threads**. Follow the promised offspring from Eden to the future, the world powers of Daniel, the kings of the north and south, Daniel's time prophecies, the covenants, Messianic prophecies, and fulfilled prophecies about nations.
4. **30-minute read:** individual events, dates exactly as printed, scriptures and images.

A layout that only works at level 4 fails.

### The density problem: the main design challenge

| period | records | character |
|---|---|---|
| 4026–2000 B.C.E. | ≈ 26 | 2,000 years, very sparse: long overlapping lives |
| 2000–1000 B.C.E. | ≈ 127 | patriarchs, Exodus, judges |
| 1000–1 B.C.E. | ≈ 300 | kings, prophets, exile, world powers: medium and parallel tracks |
| 1–99 C.E. | ≈ 290 | **extremely dense**: 29–33 C.E. (Jesus' ministry) and the apostles |
| 100–1799 C.E. | ≈ 190 | sparse: apostasy, Bible translation and preservation |
| 1800–2026 | ≈ 840 | **very dense**: modern history, with about 20 per year at the peaks |
| Future | 20 | ordered, undated |

**A uniform time scale cannot work.** The layout must use variable scales, segments, insets, magnifiers or a shape that gives dense periods more room. It must stay honest about scale with visible scale breaks or labelled scale changes.

## 5. Layout concepts to explore (please wireframe 3–4 of these, or better ideas)

Not a plain left-to-right line or a plain up-down list. We want a composition that is itself memorable.

**A. River of time.**
- History is a flowing river, widening where events are dense and narrowing where they are sparse.
- It splits into two streams for Judah and Israel (997 B.C.E.). The Israel stream dries up in 740; the Judah stream falls into exile in 607 and returns in 537.
- World powers are banks or landmasses the river passes.
- It thins to a thread through the apostasy centuries, widens again from 1870, and flows into a "future" lake or garden.
- Series are coloured currents inside the river.

**B. Serpentine road / metro map.**
- Time snakes across the sheet in rows (boustrophedon), and each row can have its own scale.
- Series are metro lines running in parallel. Events are stations. An event that belongs to several series is an interchange; Jesus' death sits on seven lines.
- Prophecy-to-fulfillment arcs cross between rows.
- Labels stay horizontal.

**C. Overview strip plus magnifier panels.**
- One continuous band of all 6,000 years at a single scale runs across the top or middle for the 3-second read.
- Funnel lines lead to large magnified panels: the patriarchs' lives, the kings and prophets (A6-style twin tracks), Jesus' ministry (29–33 C.E. by Passover), the modern era by decade, and the future.

**D. Spiral or nautilus.**
- Time spirals outward from Creation, with each turn on a different scale, ending at 2026 on the outer edge and the future beyond.
- It must keep labels readable: horizontal text, with leader lines.

**E. Radial or other.**
- Free choice, if it beats A–D on the criteria in §10.

For each concept, show how it handles:
- the density profile in §4;
- the twin-track kings with the prophets;
- 4,000-year arcs;
- the future section;
- where the images go;
- where the legend and title go.

## 6. Series: the multi-series layer

Each series gets its **own colour and line style**, and must be traceable across the whole sheet. The 7 highlight series:

| series | what it shows | visual idea |
|---|---|---|
| Promised offspring (Gen. 3:15) | 7 steps from Eden (a. 4026 B.C.E.) → Abraham 1943 → David a. 1070 → Jesus 29 / 33 C.E. → Satan hurled down c. 1914 → future | a golden thread through the whole poster |
| World powers foretold by Daniel | Babylon, Medo-Persia, Greece, Rome, Anglo-America, then the stone (God's Kingdom); symbols: metals of the Daniel 2 image, beasts of Daniel 7, ram and goat of Daniel 8 | a band of power blocks, with a **statue graphic** whose parts link to the blocks |
| Kings of the north and south (Dan. 11) | rival kings from the Ptolemies and Seleucids to the 20th–21st century | two interweaving lines |
| Daniel's time prophecies | seven times (607 B.C.E.–1914), 70 weeks (455 B.C.E.–36 C.E.), 1,260 / 1,290 / 1,335 / 2,300 days | measured brackets or rulers |
| Covenants | Edenic promise, Abrahamic, Law, Davidic, priest like Melchizedek, new covenant, Kingdom covenant | cards or seals with connecting lines |
| Messianic prophecies | 27 prophecies → fulfillments in Jesus' life | many arcs converging on 29–33 C.E. |
| Fulfilled prophecies about nations | Babylon, Cyrus named, Tyre, Nineveh, Edom, Jerusalem 607 B.C.E. and 70 C.E. … | arcs from prophet to event |

Other series give structure rather than colour threads: kings of Judah and Israel, prophets and kings (NWT App. A6), patriarchs' lives, Watch Tower presidents, Governing Body, Roman emperors, Persian rulers, the 66 Bible books, and the Messiah's family line.

The wireframes should test **at least three series drawn at once**, so we can see whether threads stay legible where they cross a dense area.

## 8. Visual language (direction, not final)

- **Colour:** rich and colourful but disciplined.
  - Four era hues as large fields: suggest deep blue for Bible, warm ochre for church history, teal or green for modern, violet or gold-white for Future.
  - Seven distinct series colours that stay distinguishable over every era field, and in print.
  - Colour is never the only carrier of meaning: line style and glyph shape back it up.
- **Glyph system:**
  - Points: solid dot = exact, hollow = about (c.), half-dot = before/after, dot with whisker = within a window, dashed outline = year not stated.
  - Span ends: solid, soft, open bracket, dashed, or fade-out (ongoing).
- **Typography:** one family with excellent Tamil support, such as Noto Sans / Noto Sans Tamil or a better pairing you propose. Use tabular figures for dates. Keep a strong size hierarchy so the three reading distances work.
- **Images:**
  - Consistent frames (circle or rounded rectangle) with a thin era-coloured ring and a one-line caption.
  - Images are jw.org artwork; credit them in the footer.
  - Don't recolour artwork heavily.
- **Texture:** optional subtle paper or parchment field in the Bible era, cleaner in the modern era. Never at the cost of legibility.
- **Connectors:**
  - Leader lines from labels to points.
  - Arcs for prophecy → fulfillment: thin and tapered, in the series colour.
  - Brackets for time prophecies.

### Bilingual strategy (test in the wireframe)

Option 1 is **two editions**, English and Tamil, with the same layout and the text swapped. Option 2 is **one bilingual edition** with English primary and Tamil secondary underneath. Show one dense crop in Tamil so we can judge the expansion.

## 10. How the concepts will be judged

| criterion | question |
|---|---|
| Three reading levels | Does it work at 3 m, 1 m and 30 cm? |
| Density | Do 29–33 C.E. and 1900–2026 fit without clutter, while the sparse first 2,000 years don't look empty? |
| Honest scale | Can a reader tell where time is compressed or stretched? |
| Series visibility | Can you follow the golden thread of Gen. 3:15 from start to end? Do three threads stay legible together? |
| Comparison | Can you see who lived at the same time, and which prophet served under which king? |
| Beauty | Would a family proudly hang it? Is it colourful but calm? |
| Images | Do 20–40 images enrich it without breaking the structure? |
| Bilingual | Does Tamil fit? |
| Printability | Is it vector-friendly and CMYK-safe, with nothing critical in the bleed? |
| Extensibility | Data will grow (more events, more series, tiers). Does the layout absorb that? |
